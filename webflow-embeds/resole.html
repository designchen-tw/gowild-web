CLIMBING SHOES · MADE TO CLIMB, MADE TO LAST

攀岩鞋的製造與修補The making and remaking of a climbing shoe

從鞋楦、橡膠到換底，認識一雙攀岩鞋如何成形，也理解它如何陪伴下一段攀登。From lasts and rubber to resoling: how a climbing shoe takes shape, and how repair can extend its journey.

一雙攀岩鞋的故事，從腳與岩壁接觸的地方開始。細小的邊緣、傾斜的岩面，對鞋子的支撐與貼合提出不同要求；鞋型、材料與結構，便圍繞這些需求逐步發展。The story of a climbing shoe begins where a foot meets the wall. A small edge and a sloping surface place different demands on support and contact. Shape, materials and construction respond to those demands.

攀岩鞋以貼合的鞋身與專用橡膠，協助腳在岩點上施力。挑選時應確認整體包覆與實際踩點感受；尺碼因品牌與鞋型而異，沒有一律縮小半碼或一碼的規則，也不應以疼痛作為合腳的標準。A close fit and purpose-made rubber help the foot apply force to holds. Judge fit by how the whole shoe holds the foot and feels under load. Sizing varies by brand and model; there is no universal downsizing rule, and pain is not a measure of a good fit.

FROM LAST TO SHOE

從一個鞋楦開始It begins with a last

鞋楦是製鞋時放入鞋內的立體模具，決定鞋子的基本形狀。把材料包覆在鞋楦上，原本平面的裁片便逐漸成為能容納腳掌的空間。A last is the three-dimensional form around which a shoe is built. As materials are shaped over it, flat panels become a volume that can hold a foot.

以 La Sportiva 公開的製造流程為例，鞋面與橡膠先經裁切及準備，鞋面縫合後套上鞋楦，再黏合包邊與外底，最後研磨接合邊緣並檢查成品。每個步驟都在處理材料與形狀的關係；即使使用同一種橡膠，組裝方式不同，完成的鞋也會不同。In La Sportiva’s published factory process, upper and rubber components are prepared and cut, the upper is stitched and lasted, and rands and soles are bonded. Edges are then ground and blended before inspection. The process brings materials and shape together; the same rubber alone does not make two shoes alike.

製鞋也從使用者的回饋中發展。TENAYA 的品牌歷史提到，創辦人的攀登經驗與製鞋設計背景，逐步成為產品開發的方向。從岩壁上的需求到可以重複生產的鞋型，設計與製造之間需要持續修正。Shoe design also develops through use. TENAYA’s account connects its founder’s climbing experience with his background in footwear development. Turning a need on the wall into a repeatable product brings design and manufacturing into an ongoing conversation.

DIFFERENT ZONES, DIFFERENT ROLES

同一雙鞋，也能搭配不同材料Different materials within one shoe

TENAYA 的 Tarifa 官方資料提到，鞋子不同部位採用不同橡膠配方，分別回應小邊緣與腳跟接觸等需求。外底的名稱，並不代表整雙鞋的每個部位都使用相同材料。TENAYA explains that Tarifa uses different rubber formulations in different areas, responding to demands such as small edges and heel contact. An outsole compound name does not describe every rubber component in a shoe.

品牌也會使用自己的橡膠配方，例如 SCARPA 的 S72。比較時，還要一起看鞋底厚度、中底、整體鞋型與使用者需求，不能只靠橡膠名稱判斷一雙鞋的表現。Brands also use their own compounds, such as SCARPA’s S72. Consider outsole thickness, midsole, overall shape and intended use alongside the compound name.

換底時改用不同配方或厚度，應先與修補者討論可能的腳感變化，以及是否適合原有結構。When changing compound or thickness during a resole, discuss the expected change in feel and compatibility with the existing construction.

A SECOND LIFE

修補，是理解原本的結構Resoling starts with the existing shoe

使用後，鞋頭與前掌的外底會逐漸磨耗。若在磨損深入包邊之前處理，通常能減少需要重建的部分；等到包邊甚至鞋面破損，修補的範圍與難度也會增加。The outsole wears with use, particularly around the forefoot and toe. Repairing before wear reaches the rand can limit the work required. Damage to the rand or upper makes the job more involved.

換底不只是選一片新橡膠。原有結構、材料與工具都影響可修復性；SCARPA 的修補計畫，也將可維修設計、原有材料、工具與技術訓練放在一起看待。A resole involves more than choosing new rubber. Existing construction, materials and tools affect repairability. SCARPA’s repair programme connects repairable design with appropriate materials, equipment and training.

  1. 外底換底Outsole resole

    磨耗主要位於外底時，更換磨損橡膠，並檢查外底與包邊的接合。實際更換範圍依鞋型及磨耗情況評估。When wear is mainly in the outsole, the worn rubber is replaced and its junction with the rand checked. The scope depends on the model and condition.

  2. 鞋頭與包邊修補Toe and rand repair

    包邊磨薄或破損時，需要另行評估。送修前確認是否包含鞋頭重建，以及修補可能對貼合感造成的改變。A thin or damaged rand needs a separate assessment. Ask whether toe reconstruction is included and how the repair may affect fit.

  3. 鞋型與整體狀況Shape and overall condition

    鞋楦或成形支撐是維持形狀的重要工具，但修補也有原有磨耗的限制。能否再修，不宜只按修補次數判斷，應檢視鞋面、接合與穿著狀況。Lasts or shaping supports help control shape, but existing wear limits the result. Assess the upper, bonding and fit rather than relying on a fixed number of repairs.

La Sportiva 的保養與換底文章也指出，鞋頭穿孔後需要先重建橡膠邊緣，再裝上新外底，這可能改變表現。因此，確認鞋子剩下哪些可保留的結構，比只選一種橡膠更重要。La Sportiva also notes that a hole at the toe requires the rubber edge to be rebuilt before a new sole is fitted, which may affect performance. Assessing the structure that can be retained is an essential part of the repair decision.

CARE & REPAIR

保養、送修與修補推薦Care, repair and resolers

送修前，可以拍下鞋底、鞋頭正面、內外側與破損近照，註明品牌、鞋款、尺碼、過去修補紀錄，以及希望保留或改善的腳感。讓修補者先評估，比只問「能不能補」更容易確認期待。Before sending shoes in, photograph the sole, toe, both sides and damaged areas. Include the model, size, repair history and what you hope to preserve or change. This gives the repairer a clearer starting point.

  1. 材料與厚度Materials and thickness

    使用哪一種橡膠？有哪些厚度可選？是否適合這個鞋款與攀登需求？Which compound and thickness will be used, and why do they suit this shoe and its intended use?

  2. 處理範圍與鞋型Scope and shape

    只換外底，還是需要修補包邊？如何支撐與維持鞋型？是否有同鞋款的修補經驗？Is this an outsole resole or does the rand also need work? How will shape be supported, and has the repairer worked on this model?

  3. 費用、時間與後續Cost, turnaround and follow-up

    確認估價、預計工期，以及收到鞋後若出現接合或貼合問題，可以如何處理。Confirm the estimate, turnaround and follow-up process for bonding or fit concerns.

日常保養可以從陰涼通風與避熱開始。攀登後讓鞋子自然乾燥，避免放在曝曬車內、直接日曬或靠近熱源；清潔方式則依品牌指引處理。For everyday care, let shoes air-dry in a shaded, ventilated place. Avoid hot cars, direct sun and heat sources, and follow the brand’s cleaning guidance.

攀岩鞋從製造到修補，始終圍繞同一件事：讓材料、形狀與腳掌協同工作。了解這些關係，能幫助我們在磨耗發生時，做出更合適的維修選擇。Making and repairing a climbing shoe share a common concern: how materials, shape and the foot work together. Understanding that relationship helps inform repair decisions as the shoe wears.
